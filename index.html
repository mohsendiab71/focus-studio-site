Privacy Policy for Focus Studio

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Studio handles your data. We designed Focus Studio with a strict offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture to ensure your recordings and media files remain entirely under your personal control.

1. Local-First Media and Processing

Focus Studio operates as a local Windows desktop application.

  • All screen capture operations, audio inputs (microphone and system audio), webcam feeds, and exported media files are rendered, encoded, and saved exclusively on your local system storage.
  • We do not operate cloud storage servers for user media.
  • No video or audio recordings are uploaded, analyzed, synced, or transmitted to any remote servers.

2. Licensing and Purchases

  • Payment Processing: All payment transactions are managed directly and securely by Gumroad, Inc., a PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processor. Focus Studio does not collect, process, or store financial account numbers, credit card details, or billing addresses.
  • License Verification: When activating Focus Studio, the application sends your user-provided License Key over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Gumroad's API (api.gumroad.com) strictly to verify the purchase validity and state. After successful validation, activation state is retained locally on your computer to enable offline use without recurring checks.

3. Analytics and Telemetry

Focus Studio does not incorporate covert behavioral trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, keyloggers, or advertising identifiers. The software executes its core screen recording tasks without monitoring background user activity or personal file systems.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because your recording files are stored strictly on your local computer, you have total control over retention, backups, and deletion of all created content. We implement standard platform security practices to ensure local settings and validation data remain isolated to standard user profile application directories.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this policy if technical modifications to licensing or functional capabilities require operational changes. The latest revision date will always be indicated at the top of this document.
